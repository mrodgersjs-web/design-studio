# SECTION CATALOG v1 — 54 clickable options, 12 slots, 2 global systems

The composable build library. Every variant = conversion structure (CONVERSION-PATTERNS.md)
+ craft surface. Two global systems re-theme every selection:

## Global systems
**Motion Narrative (4 levels)** — `still` (print-grade, reduced-motion parity) · `ambient`
(loops, pausable, offscreen-paused) · `scrubbed` (scroll = reversible state; scrubbed numerals,
clocks, cameras) · `cinematic` (pinned chapters + continuous camera; budget-gated).
**Layout Mode (4)** — `grid` (12-col discipline) · `editorial` (asymmetric magazine spreads,
marginal notes) · `organic` (flowing overlap: curved dividers, broken-grid placements, collage
layers — controlled chaos on the same tokens) · `instrument` (rail + coordinates + data chrome).
**Theme seeds**: `traject-cream` (Fraunces/amber/ink) · `noir` (dark/ember) · `paper-mono`.

## The 12 slots × 54 variants

### 1. hero (6)
- `hero-cinematic-film` — full-bleed scrubbed film + outcome H1 overlay. Motion: ambient→cinematic. Use when the product IS a feeling. Conversion: keep the 8-second answers over the film.
- `hero-instrument-deck` — the Traject winner: rail + coordinates + H1 + price line. Motion: scrubbed numerals. Best for operator/technical audiences.
- `hero-editorial-split` — magazine split: serif H1 left, product visual right, trust strip below. Fastest to make credible.
- `hero-type-monument` — type-only, no imagery (evidence: stock removal can outperform on B2B). Variable-font kinetics carry the wow.
- `hero-product-first` — the app IS the hero (proof strip as first viewport). Best when the UI is the differentiator (+ our flight-deck evidence).
- `hero-organic-collage` — layered collage: plates, cards, type overlapping at angles (organic mode's flagship). High wow, needs contrast discipline.

### 2. narrative (5)
- `narrative-pinned-chapters` — pinned scenes, one idea each, continuous camera (our Q12 gate).
- `narrative-reversible-states` — every visual state = f(scroll), reversible (Exat pattern).
- `narrative-day-arc` — time-of-day progression as story spine (our dawn system).
- `narrative-zigzag-moments` — alternating media/copy moments (classic; use varied copy budgets or it templates).
- `narrative-manifesto-scroll` — long-form serif essay with marginal artifacts; doctrine products.

### 3. product (5)
- `product-deck-strip` — compact real-UI frame with caption (our t≈30s proof strip).
- `product-bento-lite` — bento ONLY for dashboards/feature grids (never marketing heroes — cohort tell).
- `product-tabbed-rooms` — tabbed product surfaces (Today/Jobs/People pattern).
- `product-before-after` — split comparison (draft vs sent, chaos vs deck).
- `product-data-wall` — live metrics wall (real numbers only; honest sources).

### 4. proof (5)
- `proof-artifact-frames` — treated mini-frames of real artifacts (our i4 winner).
- `proof-metrics-band` — 3–4 named numbers, sources noted (+22% pattern).
- `proof-named-claims` — "Used by 8 of the Fortune 50" style; REAL names only or don't ship.
- `proof-case-timeline` — one customer's week, timestamped (real data only).
- `proof-founding-ledger` — "first 50 runs watched by hand; numbers publish here" (honest pre-traction proof).

### 5. media (4)
- `media-scrubbed-cinema` — scroll drives film clock.
- `media-ambient-loop` — poster + pause + IO-offscreen-pause + reduced still (the law).
- `media-poster-reveal` — still plate that reveals film on interaction (click/visible).
- `media-pip-stories` — small labeled PiP chips (our Atlas chip; label or it dies).

### 6. pricing (5)
- `pricing-tier-cards` — 3 tiers, one recommended, anchor line (our winner).
- `pricing-boarding-table` — ticket/boarding-pass table (compact, mobile-stacking).
- `pricing-slider` — usage slider with live price (usage-priced products).
- `pricing-tier-bench` — tiers + service bench (productized-service; honest bar, no fake humans).
- `pricing-single-manifesto` — one price, one page of conviction.

### 7. objections (4)
- `objections-accordion` — classic FAQ; keep ≤6, real questions.
- `objections-skeptic-qa` — objection-led ("Is this just another AI tool?") with receipts.
- `objections-myth-reality` — two-column myth/receipt.
- `objections-inline-adjacent` — one objection answered within a viewport of every CTA.

### 8. team (8)… see crew: 4
- `team-roster-ledger` — first-4-full + manifest grid (our compressed roster).
- `team-manifest-grid` — name+role grid only (compact).
- `team-cards-wall` — full cards (only with real people/photos).
- `team-org-orbit` — orbital org diagram (organic mode).

### 9. ask (4)
- `ask-mechanics-list` — Day 0/1/14 numbered mechanics (kills "what happens next").
- `ask-minimal` — one line + one button + risk reversal.
- `ask-sticky-rail` — sticky-bottom CTA (+11% evidence).
- `ask-application` — short application form (2–4 fields; qualify, don't gatekeep).

### 10. footer (4)
- `footer-encore` — designed ending: one line, one CTA, signature (peak-end rule).
- `footer-sitemap` — utility columns.
- `footer-signature` — coordinates/edition mark (brand signature; decode it on-site).
- `footer-minimal` — legal line only.

### 11. rails (4)
- `rail-sticky-cta` — header CTA appearing after first scroll.
- `rail-exit-honest` — scroll-away recap strip (NOT a modal; honesty-audit safe).
- `rail-inline-optin` — inline email capture with specific promise.
- `rail-announcement` — top bar for launches/status.

### 12. trust (4)
- `trust-security-band` — SOC2/privacy claims WITH evidence links (no badge theater).
- `trust-privacy-explainer` — "what stays on your machine" diagram (network-verifiable claims).
- `trust-press-logos` — only real coverage, linked.
- `trust-status-changelog` — live status + dated changelog (builds engineering trust).

## Composition presets (evidence stacks)
- **converter** (+22/+11 stack): hero-editorial-split → proof-named-claims → product-deck-strip →
  pricing-tier-cards → objections-inline-adjacent → ask-sticky-rail → footer-encore.
- **flagship** (award-first): hero-instrument-deck → narrative-day-arc → media-scrubbed-cinema →
  proof-artifact-frames → pricing-tier-cards → ask-mechanics-list → footer-signature.
- **organic** (layout=organic everywhere): hero-organic-collage → narrative-manifesto-scroll →
  product-before-after → proof-artifact-frames → pricing-boarding-table → ask-minimal → footer-encore.
